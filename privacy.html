Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

  • Account information: your name, work email, and team details when you sign up.
  • Incident data: alerts, schedules, escalation policies, notes, and status page content you create in OnCallIQ. This is your data — we process it only to run the service.
  • Usage data: basic logs (pages visited, API calls, timestamps) used for reliability and abuse prevention.

AI features

Our AI-assisted postmortem feature sends incident timelines to the Anthropic API (Claude) to generate draft summaries. Data sent to Anthropic is processed under Anthropic's terms and is not used to train their models. AI features never publish or page anyone without your team's explicit approval.

How we use data

  • To operate, maintain, and improve OnCallIQ.
  • To send service notifications (alerts, schedule changes, security notices).
  • To respond to support requests.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use incident data for advertising.

Subprocessors

We rely on a small number of providers: cloud hosting, email delivery, and Anthropic for AI features. Each processes data only as instructed by us.

Retention

Incident data is kept for the life of your account, or per your plan's retention setting on paid tiers. When you delete your account, we delete your data within 30 days, except where law requires longer retention.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to production systems is limited to the engineers who need it. If we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you promptly.

Your rights

You can request a copy, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time by writing to hello@xbyteid.codes.
