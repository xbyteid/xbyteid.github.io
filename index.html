Powered by Claude

Postmortems That Write Their First Draft.

When an incident closes, OnCallIQ sends the timeline — alerts, acknowledgements, notes, and resolution steps — to Claude, by Anthropic, via the Anthropic API. Claude drafts a structured postmortem: what happened, the likely root cause, contributing factors, and concrete follow-up actions.

Your team edits and approves before anything is shared. Claude never pages anyone, never resolves an incident, and never publishes on its own — it just clears the blank page so the retrospective actually gets written.

  • ✓Timeline summaries generated from the real incident record, not guesswork.
  • ✓Root-cause suggestions with the evidence that supports them.
  • ✓Action items extracted and assigned to owners you confirm.
  • ✓Human approval required before any postmortem is published.

Founded March 2025

OnCallIQ started after one too many 3am pages at our previous jobs. We kept asking why incident tooling had to mean per-alert pricing, year-long contracts, and a sales call just to try the product. So we built the focused alternative we wished existed: routing, schedules, and status pages that a small team can set up in an afternoon — with AI doing the tedious part of the postmortem, and humans making every real decision.

We’re independent, remote across three time zones, and growing steadily with early-stage engineering teams who’d rather be shipping than babysitting their pager.
